Strategic Advisory for Strategy & Execution

For leadership teams that need a seasoned outside brain on retainer, and a partner who stays through execution.

Tell me about your business: what’s working, what isn’t, and where you’re getting stuck. I’ll help you examine the options, spot problems early, and work out a practical next step.

Sometimes the challenge is how the team works together. I bring an outside perspective to the conversations, decisions, and responsibilities that affect whether a good plan gets carried out.

You get answers in hours, introductions when they matter, and an advisor whose incentives you never question.

A retainer gives you an experienced advisor to call when decisions come up, with access to specialist help when the work calls for it.

AI, Applied

Practical AI for your business. First, I put AI to work on your behalf. Then I help your team learn where it can be useful.

We can start with a market and competitive research brief, or a review of how AI assistants currently describe your brand and category. I check the findings against the available sources and use them to help you decide what deserves attention.

Straight answers

Questions We Answer

How do you help an ingredient company enter the US market?

I help you clarify the regulatory requirements with qualified specialists, define your positioning, and plan the channel and partner conversations. The order depends on your ingredient, evidence, and readiness for the market.

Can you help an international ingredient company find US partners?

Yes. I help identify appropriate partners, prepare the introduction, and support the evaluation and commercial discussions. I work with companies entering the US market as well as those already operating here.

How do I turn clinical research into a brand?

Start with what the research supports and why it matters to your customer. I help turn that evidence into clear positioning, a recognizable brand, and a story customers can understand and trust.

Why is my clinically studied ingredient not selling?

Good evidence is one part of the commercial picture. I look at positioning, pricing, channels, customer needs, and the steps taken so far to identify what may be holding sales back.

Can you be a sounding board for a small marketing team?

Yes. An advisory retainer gives you an experienced outside perspective on priorities and decisions, with the scope and response expectations agreed at the start.

Can you lead marketing and business development for an ingredient brand?

Through a client representation engagement, I can coordinate agreed marketing and business development responsibilities and bring in specialists as needed. We define the scope, decision authority, and expectations together.

Why isn’t ChatGPT recommending my supplement brand?

There may be several reasons. I review how AI assistants describe your brand and category, then look for gaps in the accessible information, evidence, and clarity of your positioning. Recommendations vary; no one can guarantee inclusion.

Do you offer AI search optimization for supplement companies?

Yes. I help improve the clarity, consistency, and sourcing of information about your ingredient or brand, and review how it appears in AI-assisted search. The work supports accurate discovery without promising a particular citation or ranking.

Should we build AI capability in-house or outsource it?

That depends on your team, workload, and goals. I help you test useful applications, identify the review they require, and decide what your team should own and where outside support makes sense.

How do you position an ingredient against commodity competitors?

I start with the differences your evidence supports and the value those differences offer a customer. Then I help make that value clear in the brand, sales materials, and conversations with prospective buyers.

Can you help when our commercial team isn’t working well together?

I can offer an outside perspective on priorities, communication, and decision-making. Together, we look at what is slowing the work and clarify what needs to change.

More questions

What is HealthCar?

HealthCar is my advisory practice for nutrition and health companies. I help with commercial strategy, execution, partnerships, and practical applications of AI.

Do you advise nutraceutical and functional ingredient companies?

Yes. I help ingredient companies clarify their commercial strategy and stay involved as the agreed work moves into execution, bringing in additional expertise where it is needed.

How do I get brands to formulate with my ingredient?

Give the brand a clear reason to consider it: a relevant benefit, supporting evidence, and a practical fit for its product. I help develop that case and identify appropriate companies for the conversation.

What does a US branded ingredient launch involve?

It starts with understanding the product, evidence, regulatory requirements, and customer. From there, I help plan positioning, channel priorities, partner outreach, and marketing activity in an order that fits the business.

How can I improve the chance that AI assistants cite my ingredient?

Make information about it clear, consistent, accessible, and well supported. I help assess your existing materials and identify improvements. Those steps can support discoverability, but platforms decide which sources to retrieve and cite.

Do you train supplement marketing teams to use AI?

Yes. I start with relevant work, demonstrate useful applications, and help your team learn the tools and review practices needed to use them responsibly.

What does a strategic advisor do for a nutrition company?

I help you decide where to focus, plan the next steps, and coordinate the people needed to carry them out. The engagement can include ongoing advice, project support, or client representation.

How do your industry introductions work?

I start with the business need, identify people who may be a good fit, and make an introduction with context. Where the engagement calls for it, I stay involved as the opportunity develops.

Do you work with clients over the long term?

Yes. Some relationships have continued for more than 25 years. The work evolves as the company’s needs change, with the scope agreed along the way.

Who is Mike Danielson?

I’m the founder of HealthCar, with 35 years of experience helping nutrition and health companies bring ingredients and products to market.
