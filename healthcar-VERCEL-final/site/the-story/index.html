The Story

I asked people when they’d seen me at my best.

After years of helping other companies explain their value, I wanted a clearer understanding of my own. So I asked clients and colleagues for specific examples of me at my best, and what those moments had meant to them.

25

Clients, partners, and colleagues of 20-plus years were asked.

21

Responded.

3

Questions, ten minutes per person.

The exercise

Three questions

Everyone answered the same three questions. Each took about ten minutes.

01

What strengths did you see?

02

When did you see those strengths in action?

03

What difference did they make?

This website grew out of those examples.

Their repeated words

  • Connector
  • Generative optimism
  • Speed
  • Foresight
  • Generosity

What came back

The responses described someone who connects people, sees possibilities, and helps stalled work move forward. They gave me a clearer way to explain what I bring to a business relationship. This website grew out of those examples.

Try it yourself

If you’d like to try the same exercise for yourself or your company, I’m happy to explain how I approached it. Specific examples from people who know your work can reveal value you’ve stopped noticing.
