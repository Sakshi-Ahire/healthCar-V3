In my own words

When I am at my best, I see the connection before anyone else does: the right expert for the stubborn problem, the right partner for the ingredient nobody knows yet, the right words for the science a brand cannot explain. I help people see what’s possible, then work with them to move it forward. And I stay involved when it’s time to put the plan into practice.

I asked clients and colleagues to describe me at my best, using the Reflected Best Self exercise described in Harvard Business Review. Twenty-one people responded. Their examples helped me put words to the work I’d been doing for years.

My background spans 35 years in nutrition and health, including hundreds of product launches and dozens of branded ingredients across retail, e-commerce, and healthcare practitioner channels. Today, through HealthCar, I advise ingredient innovators and health brands on building their businesses in the US market.

The order matters. Understand the science and what you can say about it. Give customers a clear reason to care. Build the brand and choose the channels before spending heavily on promotion. I help you work out what needs to happen first, so effort and money go toward the right next step.

The name

Why the name HealthCar

I bought my first Porsche for $2,300, fixed it up, sold it, and kept going: fixing, tuning, trading up for decades, until the day I drove my dream car, a new high-performance 911 Carrera, and could honestly say the cash I ever laid out was $2,300 rolled forward.

That is how I treat companies. Find what is extraordinary under the hood, get it running the way it was built to run, and keep rolling the gains forward. Health plus Car is not a quirky name. It is the method.

One car led to the next.

From the first Porsche to the Carrera, each gain helped fund the next step.

Before HealthCar

The agency era

I spent three decades building and leading the health and nutrition practice of a national media relations agency, working shoulder to shoulder with the industry’s founding generation. Three years ago I founded HealthCar to serve companies beyond publicity: strategy and execution across the whole commercialization journey. Many of my clients still work with my former agency for publicity and digital, and I like it that way.

Helping first

Away from the desk

And the part no one pays for but everyone mentions: I genuinely want the people around me to win. Thirty-five years in, the network that built my business was built by helping first. Still is. Give me a garden, a garage, or a country I have not seen, and I am happy.

Purpose

Why I do this

Happiness is enjoyment plus satisfaction plus meaning, in that order.

Early in my career, a physician client told me something I never forgot: in his office he could help a dozen patients a day, but the media work we did together reached hundreds of thousands of people a week, in a voice they trusted, waking people up to become captains of their own healthcare. I have been chasing that multiplication ever since.

In 1993, the call was carrying trusted voices to millions. Today, it is carrying trusted companies into the AI platforms where their customers now search, and trusted judgment into their rooms.

And what do I get out of it? A formula a friend taught me: happiness is enjoyment plus satisfaction plus meaning, in that order. This work pays all three. I enjoy the people. I find satisfaction watching believed-in projects outrun funded ones. And the meaning arrives every time a trajectory changes because the right people found each other at the right table.
